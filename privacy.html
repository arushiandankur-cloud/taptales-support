Privacy policy

TapTales for iPhone and iPad · Last updated 20 September 2026

TapTales is for parents to make and read picture stories with a small child. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not ask you to make an account. This page says what leaves your device and why.

Stays on your device and in your iCloud

  • Your stories, pictures and recordings. They are stored on your device and synced through your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit). We never receive them and can't read them.
  • Reading a story aloud. When you use “Read one aloud”, speech is turned into text on your device. Your reading isn't sent anywhere.
  • Settings and progress. Kept on the device.

What our server receives

TapTales Pro can make a storyteller voice for a story. To do that, the app sends:

  • The words of the story, a page at a time, when you tap Narrate. Our server passes them to a voice provider, ElevenLabs, which returns the audio. We do not keep the text after the audio is made. The provider handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • An install identifier that Apple gives every app from us (the vendor identifier). We use it to count how many narrated stories a free or paid account has used, and to refuse requests over the limit. It is not linked to your name and can't identify you outside the app.
  • Your subscription receipt, signed by Apple, so the server knows whether Pro is active.
  • A story identifier and a month, to count stories against your monthly allowance.

We use this only to run the app. We don't sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to track you across other apps or sites.

Family sharing

If you add someone in Settings → Family sharing, the stories you choose are shared with that person through Apple's iCloud sharing. The people you share with can see and, if you allow it, add to those stories, including the names on recordings. Sharing is between your iCloud accounts. We don't receive it.

Permissions

  • Microphone, to record a page or a whole story in your voice.
  • Speech recognition, to turn a story you read aloud into tiles, on the device.
  • Face ID, only if you switch on the option that asks for it before a story closes. TapTales never sees your face data.

Children

TapTales is used by children but managed by a parent or guardian, who writes the stories and holds the subscription. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only identifiers we receive come from the parent's device, as described above.

Deleting your data

Delete a story in the app and it leaves your iCloud. Delete the app and iCloud data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage. The identifier and story counts on our server contain nothing that identifies you; email us if you want them removed.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we'll update the date above. Questions: taptales@googlegroups.com.

TapTales support

Something not working, or a question about a story? Email taptales@googlegroups.com and include your iPhone or iPad model, your iOS version, and what happened. Replies usually take a day or two.

Common questions

How do I leave a story? Press and hold the small ✕ in the corner for about a second and a half. A tap does nothing, on purpose. In Settings you can also require Face ID or your passcode.

How do I keep my child inside the app? Turn on Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access). TapTales walks you through it on the home screen.

My recordings aren't on my other device. Recordings sync through your own iCloud, and large audio can take a few minutes. Check you're signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on, then pull the refresh button on the shelf.

How do I cancel TapTales Pro? On your device: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → TapTales Pro. Apple handles billing and refunds at support.apple.com/billing.

Restore a purchase. Open Settings in TapTales and tap TapTales Pro → Restore.

Delete my data. Stories and recordings are in your iCloud and on your device: delete the story in the app, or delete the app and its iCloud data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage. For anything the server holds, see the privacy policy.
